# Business Opportunity Control Rules

## Evidence and thesis

- Material statement phân `FACT`, `ESTIMATE`, `HYPOTHESIS`; có source/version/date/rights/freshness/confidence.
- Thesis đủ target customer/job/problem, current alternative/cost, value, differentiation, right-to-win, revenue/cost logic và strategic fit.
- Interest, survey intent, trend, TAM hoặc partner conversation không tự chứng minh willingness to pay/attainable demand.
- Asset/data/IP chỉ dùng khi rights rõ; không claim capability chưa có evidence/capacity.

## Assumptions and economics

- Assumptions thuộc desirability, feasibility, viability, defensibility, compliance; ghi evidence for/against, confidence, consequence, owner, testability.
- Economics có formula/inputs/sources, low/base/high, currency/base year/horizon, sensitivity, downside, capital/capacity exposure.
- Không ẩn cannibalization, dependency, opportunity cost, sunk cost hoặc legal/privacy/IP/ethics risk.

## Experiments and stage gates

- Test riskiest assumption bằng experiment nhỏ nhất tạo evidence; không test phần dễ để hợp thức hóa ý tưởng.
- Experiment có consent/rights, sample, metric/baseline, success+kill criteria, max time/budget, owner, evidence capture và stop rule.
- Criteria khóa trước kết quả; failed evidence không bị xóa; stage movement do human authority.
- Decision options gồm continue/pivot/hold/kill; recommendation không là execution authorization.

## Forbidden controls

Flags: `evidence_fabricated`, `demand_fabricated`, `pain_fabricated`, `wtp_fabricated`, `market_size_fabricated`, `price_fabricated`, `cost_fabricated`, `margin_fabricated`, `capability_fabricated`, `partner_interest_fabricated`, `validation_result_fabricated`, `rights_bypassed`, `ip_misused`, `sensitive_data_misused`, `confirmation_bias`, `contradiction_hidden`, `cannibalization_hidden`, `dependency_hidden`, `risk_hidden`, `sunk_cost_escalated`, `criteria_changed`, `failed_evidence_deleted`, `deceptive_test`, `auto_funded`, `auto_contacted`, `auto_built`, `auto_priced`, `auto_contracted`, `auto_launched`, `auto_scaled`.

States: `FUNDED`, `CONTACTED`, `BUILT`, `PRICED`, `CONTRACTED`, `LAUNCHED`, `SCALED`, `APPROVED`.

Reviews: `STRATEGY_PORTFOLIO`, `CUSTOMER_MARKET`, `FINANCE_ECONOMICS`, `DELIVERY_CAPABILITY`, `LEGAL_DATA_IP` PASS; `FINAL_HUMAN_OPPORTUNITY_DECISION` PENDING.
