# People & Talent Rules — v2.3

## 1. Thứ tự sự thật

1. Current applicable labor, employment, privacy, accessibility and anti-discrimination requirements for the entity/jurisdiction/use case.
2. Approved people policies, job architecture, competency framework, collective/employee agreements and decision-authority matrix.
3. Authorized HRIS/work records, validated assessments, work samples and versioned people evidence.
4. Consented feedback, interviews, surveys, learning and exit evidence.
5. Model inference, manager opinion and unverified profile claims — labelled, never promoted as fact.

NIST A.I RMF, official selection-procedure guidance and ILO algorithmic-management research are principle references only; they do not override local law or policy.

## 2. People decision contract

- Purpose: use case, decision stage, population, audience and prohibited uses.
- Job: purpose, outcomes, essential duties, requirements, competencies, context and evidence method.
- Governance: entity/jurisdiction/as-of, owner, decision authority, preparer/reviewer, SOD, appeal/correction/accommodation and notification.
- Data: source, rights/lawful basis/notice/consent, classification, minimization, access, retention/deletion, version and confidence.
- Evaluation: criteria, job-relatedness/validity, scale, threshold basis, missing rule, context, uncertainty, fairness and monitoring.

Missing fields stay UNKNOWN; do not silently default or rank.

## 3. Sensitive-data rules

- Do not infer or diagnose health, disability, pregnancy, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, politics, union status, mental state or personality from text, image, voice, behavior or metadata.
- Do not use protected traits or proxies for selection, rating or adverse action.
- If applicable law permits fairness audit, isolate required protected data, restrict access to authorized reviewers, aggregate results and suppress unsafe small groups.
- Salary, discipline, grievance, health, biometrics, identity and background-check data require explicit need, rights, access and retention controls.
- No public model upload or covert monitoring.

## 4. Job, assessment and evidence rules

- Every criterion must link to job outcome/duty and a defensible evidence method.
- Separate essential from preferred; credential from demonstrated capability; potential from current proficiency.
- Assessment must be accessible and offer approved accommodation; non-comparable conditions are disclosed.
- Preserve raw evidence, correction history, scorer/calibration data, model/tool/version and human overrides.
- A score supports review; it does not define a person or authorize an action.

## 5. Performance and development rules

- Metrics declare purpose, formula, grain, denominator, cohort, window, source, target basis and limitations.
- Include opportunity, resources, workload, manager/system context, role change and missing evidence.
- Feedback identifies behavior/evidence/impact; employee response, correction and appeal remain visible.
- Development plan needs gap evidence, voluntary/authorized option, access, success measure, reassessment and owner.
- Never force enrollment, fabricate rating or infer stable character.

## 6. Succession and retention rules

- Succession readiness uses role criteria, evidence, gaps, development, availability constraints and bench risk; it does not appoint a successor.
- Retention analysis starts at cohort level, declares denominator/window/missingness and alternative explanations.
- Individual attrition inference cannot drive adverse action, surveillance or retaliation.
- Support options require capacity, consent where applicable, equality of access, success/stop criteria and human owner.

## 7. Fairness and due-process rules

- Record tool/model/version, intended use, validation/job-relatedness basis, population, subgroup outcome, denominator, sample size, missingness and uncertainty.
- No universal adverse-impact formula or threshold. Use the current applicable standard and qualified review.
- Investigate disparity, proxy features, inaccessible steps, opportunity differences and reviewer calibration; preserve alternatives.
- Provide notice where required, understandable reasons, accommodation route, correction/appeal and accountable human decision.

## 8. Stop conditions

Stop at NOT_READY for missing authority/lawful basis/job contract/source rights, sensitive inference, fabricated evidence, hidden scoring, inaccessible assessment, unreviewed disparity, retaliation risk or execution request.

Final state is READY_FOR_HUMAN_PEOPLE_TALENT_DECISION; employment actions and external communication remain outside.
