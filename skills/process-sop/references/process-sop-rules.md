# Process SOP Rules

## 1. Mandate and evidence

Freeze objective/outcome, process/document type, audience, trigger/start/end, scope/volume/criticality, as-of/horizon and authorities. Current-process claims require authorized observation, interview, log, record, policy/control, incident or exception evidence. Separate actual practice, required practice, local workaround, hypothesis and contradiction.

## 2. Boundary and architecture

Map supplier, input/precondition, trigger, stages, output/acceptance, customer, upstream/downstream interface and system of record. Compare current/target flow and justify each change. Simplify waste, but do not remove a control or automate a broken process without evidence and authority.

## 3. Roles and separation of duties

Define accountable owner, performer, reviewer, approver, informed party, competence/access, backup and handoff. Use roles instead of named-person dependency. Identify incompatible duties and access; a RACI entry does not itself grant access or approval authority.

## 4. Executable steps

Each step needs ID, trigger/input, performer/accountable role, action or decision rule, output/acceptance, contextual SLA basis, system/record/evidence, control, dependency and next state. Use explicit conditions and exception routes instead of vague discretion. Do not invent a duration when no measured or approved basis exists.

## 5. Exceptions, escalation and continuity

For each material exception define condition/detection, containment, allowed deviation, escalation threshold/route/SLA, decision authority, evidence, recovery/rollback and closure. Cover system/data/people/vendor/capacity/control failures and continuity/manual fallback where relevant.

## 6. Controls and metrics

Controls specify objective, preventive/detective/corrective type, owner, event/frequency, evidence, pass/fail, failure action and residual risk. Metrics specify formula, grain, numerator/denominator, cohort/window, source/freshness, owner and target basis. Activity and speed do not replace quality, control effectiveness or customer/business outcome.

## 7. Assets and records

Attach usable forms, checklists, decision tables, scripts/examples and record fields. Define naming, version, access, retention, completion evidence and step references. Do not copy restricted content or expose real credentials/PII in examples.

## 8. Test, activation and change

Walk through happy, exception, edge, continuity and access cases with expected result/evidence, defect and retest. Training/competence and adoption evidence are separate from document quality. Version/effective date, communication, rollback and old-version treatment require human authority. The Skill cannot publish/activate/retire SOP, change workflow/system/access, execute work, mutate records, approve policy or certify training.

## 9. D10 pilot

Pilot at least three real processes with different risk: routine repeatable, exception-heavy cross-functional and controlled/data-sensitive. Compare baseline/v2.3 on execution/review comprehension, step/role/control/exception defects, cycle/quality/control metrics, UAT/adoption, deviation/incident rate, token and duration. Static PASS does not prove operational effectiveness or compliance.
