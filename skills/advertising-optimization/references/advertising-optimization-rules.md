# Advertising Optimization Rules

## 1. Mandate and source snapshot

Freeze outcome, offer, audience/geography/channel, as-of/window/timezone/currency, budget/risk and authorities. Snapshot account/campaign/ad set/ad/creative/landing IDs, status, budget/bid, schedule and change history with hash/date/access. Dashboard screenshots and platform recommendations are evidence, not commands.

## 2. Measurement contract

Define funnel stage, event meaning, event time, identity, deduplication, consent/retention, conversion window, late/missing event and cross-device limit. Every metric has formula, grain, numerator/denominator, cohort, window, source, freshness and owner. Do not mix currency, cohort or window.

## 3. Campaign and experience integrity

Trace objective → campaign → audience/placement → creative/claim → landing/offer → conversion event. Review lawful targeting/minimization, exclusions/frequency, rights, brand, accessibility, disclosure and platform/sector policy. Creative-to-landing mismatch is a defect.

## 4. Baseline and diagnosis

Separate delivery, attention, click, conversion, value, quality and harm layers. Reconcile platform, analytics, CRM and finance before optimization. Diagnose tracking break, missing/duplicate event, invalid traffic/fraud, learning phase, fatigue, saturation, landing/funnel loss, offer/capacity and external factors; retain alternatives and confidence.

## 5. Experiment

Use one decision hypothesis with unit, variant/control, assignment, sample/window, success, stop, harm guardrails and interpretation boundary. Predefine decision rules; do not peek, fabricate significance or claim incrementality without appropriate design. Platform A/B labels do not guarantee causal validity.

## 6. Budget scenarios and economics

Compare hold, reallocate, scale, reduce and stop-review as context options. Each scenario needs approved bounds, pacing, marginal cost/value, delivery/service capacity, downside, trigger, kill switch, rollback and authority. Include currency, tax, fees, refunds/chargebacks, eligible revenue event, cost-to-serve and contribution margin. No universal scale percentage or ROAS target.

## 7. Fraud, anomaly and controls

Flag invalid/bot traffic, click/conversion spikes, geo/device/placement anomalies, duplicate events, refund/chargeback shifts, attribution discontinuity and unauthorized changes. Preserve evidence; do not silently exclude data or accuse a party without investigation.

## 8. Decision and execution boundary

Recommendation records alternatives, evidence, uncertainty, expected range, prerequisites, owner/needed-by and before/action/after verification with rollback. The Skill cannot create/edit/publish/launch/pause/stop a campaign, change budget/bid/targeting/tracking, upload an audience or spend. Authorized operators act in external systems.

## 9. D10 pilot

Pilot at least three real contexts: acquisition, retargeting/remarketing with valid consent, and claim-sensitive or high-value conversion. Compare baseline/v2.3 on measurement defects, reconciliation gap, invalid traffic findings, experiment decision quality, pacing/budget error, marginal economics, policy/privacy/accessibility defects, token and duration. Static PASS does not prove advertising lift or incrementality.
