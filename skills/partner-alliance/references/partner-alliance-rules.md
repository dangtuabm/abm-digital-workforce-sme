# Partner Alliance Rules

## 1. Counterparty and mandate

Freeze alliance type, purpose, scope, geography/segment, customer outcome, as-of/horizon, budget/capacity and authorities. Resolve legal entity, ownership/control, authorized representatives and conflicts. Brand fame, audience size and informal relationship are signals, not authority/capability.

## 2. Fit and contribution

Evaluate strategy/customer fit, complementary capability, alternatives, right-to-win, delivery feasibility and customer/public impact. Contribution ledger includes asset/capability/capacity/cost/risk/rights/owner/evidence for each party. Access to customer data/list requires purpose, lawful basis/consent and minimization.

## 3. Due-diligence domains

Minimum six domains:

- `STRATEGY_CUSTOMER_FIT`
- `FINANCE_SOLVENCY_TAX`
- `LEGAL_COMPLIANCE_COMPETITION_ANTI_BRIBERY`
- `DATA_SECURITY_PRIVACY`
- `IP_BRAND_REPUTATION`
- `DELIVERY_QUALITY_CAPACITY`

Each record needs source, finding, severity, confidence, gap, mitigation, owner and gate. Critical red flags are not offset by a high composite score.

## 4. Alliance options

Referral, reseller, co-marketing, co-delivery, bundle, strategic alliance, hold and do-nothing are context options—not maturity tiers. Compare value, control, cost, liability, data/IP exposure, channel conflict, reversibility and prerequisites. Non-exclusivity is default until evidence/authority supports otherwise.

## 5. Operating and economics

Define in/out scope, roles/decision rights/RACI, service/quality/acceptance, customer/lead ownership, attribution/dispute, support, record source, change/escalation, audit and continuity.

Economics must show approved rate reference or explicit decision request; currency/tax, gross/net basis, eligible event, attribution window, refunds/clawback, cost-to-serve, contribution margin, cash timing, liability, sensitivity and authority. No universal commission/revenue-share ratio.

## 6. Competition, ethics and conflicts

Prohibit bribery/kickback, undisclosed conflict, price fixing, market/customer allocation, bid rigging, coordinated exclusion, misuse of confidential competitor information, sanctions evasion and misleading endorsement. Referral/commission must be lawful, transparent and approved.

## 7. Data, IP and brand

Specify purpose/lawful basis/consent, minimum fields, controller/processor roles if relevant, access, transfer, security, incident, retention/deletion and audit. Separate pre-existing IP, new IP, data ownership, license/territory/term, derivative rights and exit. Brand/co-marketing/publicity require approval and evidence.

## 8. Pilot and lifecycle

Pilot the riskiest assumptions with bounded scope, baseline, customer outcome, contribution, owner/capacity, success/stop/guardrails, review and rollback. Governance includes decision/issue/conflict registers, KPI contracts, cadence by context, change, renewal and exit/transition/customer continuity.

## 9. Risks

Minimum groups: `STRATEGIC_VALUE`, `COUNTERPARTY_DUE_DILIGENCE`, `DELIVERY_CUSTOMER_HARM`, `FINANCIAL_COMMERCIAL`, `LEGAL_COMPETITION_ETHICS`, `DATA_IP_BRAND_SECURITY`.

Critical defects: fabricated identity/capability/customer/access/economics, unclosed critical diligence, bribery/collusion, unlawful sharing, IP/brand misuse, hidden conflict, unauthorized outreach/offer/exclusivity/payment/agreement/signature/activation.

## 10. D10 pilot

Pilot at least three real models with different risk: referral/channel, co-delivery/co-marketing and strategic alliance. Compare baseline/v2.3 on diligence precision, value/contribution clarity, economics/attribution defects, conflict/data/IP/legal findings, pilot decision quality, customer outcome/harm, token and duration. Static self-test does not prove partner performance.
