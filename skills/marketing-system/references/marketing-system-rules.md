# Marketing System Rules

## 1. Mandate and customer truth

Freeze business/customer objective, audience and journey scope, geography/language, as-of/horizon, budget/capacity, owner and release authority. Audience insight, job, barrier and channel preference require authorized evidence with date and confidence. Trend, reach or competitor activity does not replace customer truth.

## 2. Source, claim and rights

Every material claim and content item traces to a source with locator, version/date, rights/purpose, freshness and confidence. Separate fact, approved claim, interpretation, hypothesis and unknown. Never fabricate insight, case, result, testimonial or benchmark; never copy or republish without rights. Expired or contradicted claims stop release.

## 3. Content architecture

Each pillar serves an objective, audience job/journey question and desired next behavior. A content item inherits pillar, evidence, channel contract, owner/effort/dependency, expiry and review state. The matrix exposes coverage gaps, overlap and unsupported volume; it is not a quota generator.

## 4. Channel contracts

Define audience/use case, format and platform constraints, accessibility/localization, rights/consent, CTA/choice, cadence/capacity, success metric and harm guardrail. Platform limits and policies are time-sensitive and need a current source. No universal length, cadence, hashtag or CTA rule.

## 5. Workflow and capacity

Use: brief → source/claim check → draft → fact/brand/legal/privacy/accessibility review → render/channel QA → human release → archive/learning. Specify roles, WIP, capacity, dependencies, SLA and exception route. A calendar exceeding capacity is `NOT_READY`; output volume is not an outcome.

## 6. Repurpose lineage

Maintain canonical-source and derived-asset IDs, version, preserved claim/meaning/rights, transformation, target channel, duplication guard and reviewer. Repurposing must adapt to the channel without changing the truth or hiding sponsorship/synthetic origin where disclosure is required.

## 7. Measurement and experiments

Metrics need purpose, formula, grain, denominator, cohort, window, source, freshness, baseline/target status and limitations. Separate attention, engagement, action, commercial outcome and harm. Attribution is not causality. Experiments require hypothesis, unit/audience, variant/control, assignment, sample/window, success, stop, guardrails and interpretation boundary.

## 8. Ethics, privacy and accessibility

Prohibit fake urgency/scarcity/social proof, fear or shame exploitation, discriminatory or vulnerable targeting, spam, hidden opt-out, unlawful tracking, concealed sponsorship and metric gaming. Minimize identifiers and respect consent. Accessibility and localization are release gates, not optional polish.

## 9. Activation boundary

The Skill may analyze authorized data and prepare a review pack. It cannot publish, send, schedule, buy media, enable tracking, change a campaign, launch an experiment or contact an audience. Final state remains `READY_FOR_HUMAN_CONTENT_SYSTEM_ACTIVATION` with activation `PENDING`.

## 10. D10 pilot

Pilot at least three real contexts with different risk: evergreen education, campaign/launch and regulated or claim-sensitive content. Compare baseline/v2.3 on evidence/claim defects, production lead time, capacity adherence, accessibility/rights findings, content-to-outcome traceability, harm/complaints, token cost and duration. Static self-test does not prove market response or commercial impact.
