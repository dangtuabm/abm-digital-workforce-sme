# Offer Pricing Rules

## Pricing triangulation

Use three evidence families: customer value/willingness-to-pay, alternatives/comparables and economic floor. None is sufficient alone. Record source/date/method/rights/confidence; convert conflict into a range or validation question.

## Offer and metric

Package differences must reflect real outcome, scope, entitlement, service, capacity or risk. A price metric should align with value while remaining predictable, measurable, hard to game and operationally billable.

## Economics

Separate list price, realized price, net revenue, variable cost, incremental cost, fixed allocation, gross margin, contribution margin and cash timing. State currency, tax treatment, base year, horizon, formula and sensitivity. Do not label an allocated-cost result as cash impact.

## Commercial controls

Discounts require reason code, eligibility, max rate, approver, economic-floor check, expiry and audit. Guarantees/refunds/trials require truthful claim, transparent conditions, liability/capacity assessment and legal review. Scarcity and deadlines must map to real capacity or a documented event.

## Competition and fairness

Do not coordinate price, share confidential competitor information or coerce resale price. Do not price by protected/sensitive traits or exploit vulnerability. Personalized pricing needs lawful purpose, explainable non-sensitive fences and human/legal approval.

## State policy

Publishable states: `NOT_READY`, `READY_FOR_OFFER_PRICE_REVIEW`, `READY_FOR_HUMAN_PRICE_DECISION`. Execution states `PUBLISHED`, `QUOTED`, `DISCOUNTED`, `CONTRACTED`, `INVOICED`, `CHARGED`, `PROMOTED`, `APPROVED` need external human evidence.
