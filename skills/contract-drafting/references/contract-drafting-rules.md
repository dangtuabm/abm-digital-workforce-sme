# Contract Drafting Rules — v2.3

## 1. Thứ tự sự thật

1. Current mandatory law, formalities, regulatory/tax/privacy requirements and qualified counsel for the exact transaction/jurisdiction.
2. Verified party identity/capacity/authority, approved commercial decisions and signing matrix.
3. Executed governing agreements, approved term sheet/award/negotiation record, policy and current playbook/template.
4. Authorized proposal/RFP/quote/specification and operational evidence.
5. Model clauses, prior drafts and general principles — references only, never automatically binding.

UNIDROIT Principles and UNCITRAL texts may inform issue spotting; adoption, applicability and enforceability require current legal review.

## 2. Contract mandate

- Document/deal type, purpose, transaction stage, audience, language and intended use.
- Parties/legal entities, representatives, authority evidence, notices and signature mechanics.
- Governing law, jurisdiction/forum, mandatory formality, e-sign/notarization/registration and priority language.
- Business owner, legal counsel, finance/tax/data/security/delivery reviewers and signing authority.
- Risk appetite, preferred/acceptable/fallback/redline positions, approval path and prohibited uses.

Unknown material fields stay in a controlled TBD register with owner, needed-by, consequence and blocking state.

## 3. Source and term controls

- Each source records locator/version/date/as-of/rights/confidentiality/priority/status/confidence/supports.
- Separate proposed, approved, negotiated, rejected, superseded and executed terms.
- Do not merge prior agreements, customer paper, templates or emails without precedence and rights.
- Every material clause links to commercial/legal source, owner and decision status.
- Counsel determines legal-source relevance; model-generated citations are not authority.

## 4. Commercial and obligation controls

- Scope defines deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, assumptions, roles, change and acceptance evidence.
- Payment links amount/currency/tax/expense to objective trigger, invoice/evidence, dispute and remedy.
- Obligation states actor, action/standard, trigger, due/window, dependency, evidence, exception/remedy and survival.
- Rights need not be mechanically symmetrical; allocate by role, control, value, causation and approved risk position.
- No fabricated prices, ratios, service levels, deadlines, warranties, caps or remedies.

## 5. Risk clause controls

- IP: background/foreground, license, restrictions, third-party materials, open-source if applicable and exit rights.
- Data/security: roles/instructions, purpose, location/transfer, access, retention/deletion, incident, audit and subcontractors.
- Confidentiality: scope, marking if applicable, permitted recipients, exclusions, compelled disclosure, term, return/destruction and survival.
- Warranty/remedy, indemnity, liability, insurance, compliance, audit, force majeure and dispute positions show source/rationale/risk/fallback/authority.
- A clause is not enforceable merely because it is common or included in a template.

## 6. Lifecycle and consistency controls

- Formation/offer/acceptance/effective date, performance/change, renewal, suspension, termination/cure, transition/exit and survival must align.
- Definitions, party names, roles, dates, currency/tax, milestones, acceptance/payment, notices, crossrefs, annexes and precedence must reconcile.
- Clean draft never hides unresolved redline, contradiction, TBD or reviewer gap.
- Electronic form/signature validity and automated contracting depend on applicable law, party agreement, system evidence and authority.
- Executed contract and amendments are immutable governing records; drafting never mutates them.

## 7. Negotiation and review controls

- Issue table records current text, requested text, business/legal impact, evidence, preferred/acceptable/fallback/redline, owner and authority.
- Do not negotiate or accept on behalf of a party.
- Legal privilege/confidentiality labels require policy and counsel; never claim privilege automatically.
- Six functional reviews and final legal/signing authority remain PENDING until external evidence exists.

## 8. Stop conditions

Stop at NOT_READY for missing party/authority/law/deal terms, material contradiction/TBD, unsupported risk allocation, unlawful/evasive purpose, confidentiality/rights breach or request to send/accept/negotiate/sign/execute/file.

Final state is READY_FOR_HUMAN_LEGAL_CONTRACT_DECISION; legal advice, counterpart communication and execution remain outside.
