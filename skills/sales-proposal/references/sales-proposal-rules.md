# Sales Proposal Rules

## Traceability

Every material proposal element should link: customer problem/impact/current alternative → requirement/priority → option/capability evidence → deliverable → acceptance criterion/evidence/approver. Missing links remain gaps, not polished claims.

## Scope and acceptance

Separate in-scope, out-of-scope, optional and future work. State assumptions, dependencies, customer/provider roles, service levels and change control. A milestone needs entry/exit and evidence; a calendar date is not acceptance.

## Economics and value

Use only approved, versioned price/corridor references. State currency, tax treatment, terms, validity and optional items. Customer value is a sourced range with conditions and measurement method; do not turn an estimate into a guarantee.

## Claims and proof

Capabilities, cases, credentials, benchmarks, results and availability require source/version/consent/rights. Do not reuse confidential customer evidence or manufacture social proof. Remove fixed ABM prices, payment ratios or product promises unless the current approved source explicitly authorizes them.

## Release boundary

The proposal supports a decision; it does not itself negotiate or form a contract unless legal terms expressly say so. Final review must cover customer facts, delivery, finance, commercial policy, legal/privacy/IP, brand and accessibility.

## State policy

Publishable states: `NOT_READY`, `READY_FOR_PROPOSAL_REVIEW`, `READY_FOR_HUMAN_PROPOSAL_RELEASE`. Execution states `SENT`, `QUOTED`, `NEGOTIATED`, `DISCOUNTED`, `COMMITTED`, `CONTRACTED`, `SIGNED`, `PUBLISHED`, `ACCEPTED` require external human evidence.
