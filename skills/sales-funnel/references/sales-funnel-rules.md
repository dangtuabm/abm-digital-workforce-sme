# Sales Funnel Rules

## Stage contract

A stage represents a business state, not a touch or task. It needs one declared unit, evidence-based entry/exit, valid transitions, owner/SLA, loss/recycle rule and authoritative system. Stage changes require external evidence.

## Measurement contract

Every conversion needs numerator, denominator, unit/grain, cohort, observation window, source/version, time zone and owner. Do not mix people, accounts, opportunities, orders or revenue. Reconcile eligible entries against exits, in-stage and valid loss/recycle.

## Attribution and diagnosis

Attribution assigns credit under a method; it does not prove causation. Report method and limitations. Diagnose leakage by eligible volume × rate gap × value, then distinguish measurement defects, mix shifts, capacity/SLA, qualification, offer/message and journey friction.

## Experiment

Pre-register hypothesis, audience, assignment, control/change, sample rationale, primary metric, baseline, success/kill, guardrails, duration/cap, consent/fairness and owner. Do not change criteria after observing results.

## Privacy and commercial integrity

No spam, illegal tracking, dark patterns, fabricated urgency/social proof, protected/sensitive inference or discriminatory targeting. Apply consent, retention, deletion, minimum aggregation and unsubscribe/opt-out requirements.

## State policy

Publishable states: `NOT_READY`, `READY_FOR_FUNNEL_REVIEW`, `READY_FOR_HUMAN_FUNNEL_ACTIVATION`. Execution states `PUBLISHED`, `SENT`, `ENROLLED`, `ROUTED`, `SCORED`, `ADVANCED`, `CLOSED_WON`, `ACTIVATED` require external human/system evidence.
